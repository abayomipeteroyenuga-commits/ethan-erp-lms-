DIGITAL LITERACY

FULL VISUAL STUDY MANUAL

Safety standard: use authorised accounts, harmless practice content and official recovery/support channels. Never share passwords or one-time codes, test suspicious links, expose private information, or bypass device/network restrictions.

Course Introduction

Digital Literacy develops the broad knowledge and practical judgement required to participate effectively in modern digital life. It covers devices, operating systems, internet use, search, information evaluation, communication, collaboration, digital citizenship, privacy, account security, scams, content creation, copyright, cloud work, problem solving, AI literacy and productive digital habits.

Learning Objectives

Use common digital devices and services confidently.

Search for and evaluate online information.

Communicate and collaborate professionally online.

Protect privacy, accounts and digital identity.

Recognise common phishing and scam warning signs.

Create responsible original digital content.

Respect copyright, licensing and attribution.

Apply basic cloud collaboration and digital problem solving.

Use AI tools critically and responsibly.

Lesson 1: Introduction to Digital Literacy

Introduction

Digital literacy is the ability to use digital devices, information, communication tools and online services confidently, critically, safely and responsibly.

Learning Objectives

Explain the main ideas in introduction to digital literacy.

Complete a safe guided practical for introduction to digital literacy.

Apply the lesson to a realistic learning, workplace or everyday situation.

Teaching Content

Key point: Digital literacy is broader than knowing how to operate a computer.

Key point: A digitally literate person can find information, judge its quality, create content and communicate appropriately.

Key point: Safety, privacy, ethics and responsible behaviour are part of digital competence.

Key point: Digital skills should help a learner solve real academic, workplace and everyday problems.

Course Completion Outcome

After successful completion, the learner should be able to participate confidently and responsibly in digital learning and work: use common devices and services, search and evaluate information, communicate professionally, protect privacy and accounts, recognise suspicious content, create and credit digital materials, collaborate online, solve routine digital problems and use AI tools with appropriate verification and human judgement.

Professional Practice Checklist

Verify important information before sharing it.

Use professional communication appropriate to the channel.

Protect passwords, MFA codes and private information.

Use official software and recovery processes.

Respect copyright and credit sources.

Check cloud-sharing permissions.

Review AI-generated material before using it.

Keep digital work organised and backed up.
