Course Introduction
IT Support Fundamentals prepares learners to provide safe, organised and professional first-line technology
support. The course covers service desks, ticketing, prioritisation, troubleshooting, Windows and application
support, peripherals, connectivity, accounts, email, remote support, customer service, security escalation,
knowledge bases, asset records and reporting.

Learning Objectives
 Explain the role and structure of an IT support service.
 Create clear support tickets and prioritise incidents.
 Apply a structured first-line troubleshooting method.
 Support common desktop, application, peripheral and connectivity issues safely.
 Guide legitimate account recovery without collecting user secrets.
 Communicate professionally with users.
 Recognise issues that require technical or security escalation.
 Create knowledge-base and support records.
 Operate a fictional help desk from ticket opening to closure.

Learning Objectives

 Complete a safe first-line support practical for troubleshooting methodology.

Teaching Content

Professional Practice Checklist
 Work only on authorised devices and accounts.
 Never ask for a user's password or one-time code.
 Use safe standard checks before advanced changes.
 Do not probe networks or test suspicious links/files.
 Explain actions clearly to the user.
 Document symptoms, actions and results.
 Escalate electrical, security and specialist faults promptly.
