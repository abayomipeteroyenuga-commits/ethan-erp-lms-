ETHAN DIGITAL ACADEMY

FLYER & POSTER DESIGN

FULL VISUAL STUDY MANUAL

Professional standard: use original, authorised or properly licensed assets; keep names, dates, prices, testimonials and claims accurate; protect private information; confirm commercial printer requirements before production.

Course Introduction

This course develops practical flyer and poster design skills for education, events, promotions and awareness campaigns. Learners progress from briefs and hierarchy through typography, colour, imagery, print preparation, digital adaptation, critique and final campaign production.

Learning Objectives

Interpret a design brief and audience need.

Build clear information hierarchy.

Use grids, margins, whitespace and alignment.

Apply readable typography and functional colour.

Select and crop authorised imagery.

Design event, promotional, educational and course flyers.

Prepare print-ready and digital outputs.

Create reusable campaign variations.

Complete a coordinated flyer/poster campaign.

Lesson 1: Foundations of Flyer and Poster Design

Introduction

Flyers and posters communicate a focused message through typography, imagery, colour and layout. Strong work is easy to understand at the intended viewing distance or screen size.

Learning Objectives

Explain the key principles of foundations of flyer and poster design.

Complete a guided design practical for foundations of flyer and poster design.

Apply the lesson to an Ethan Digital Academy communication task.

Teaching Content

Design principle: Begin with communication purpose before decoration.

Design principle: Prioritise the most important information and remove unnecessary clutter.

Design principle: Use consistent alignment, spacing and brand rules.

Design principle: Design for the actual output size and viewing context.

Design principle: Review accuracy, accessibility and asset permissions before approval.

Figure 1: Original instructional visual for Foundations of Flyer and Poster Design.

Step-by-Step Guided Practical

1. Write the purpose, audience and required information.

2. Sketch two rough layout options.

3. Build the selected layout with clear hierarchy and alignment.

4. Apply typography, colour and authorised imagery.

5. Review at intended size, correct errors and export a preview.

Ethan Digital Academy Case Study

Ethan Digital Academy can apply foundations of flyer and poster design to course launches, training events, learner information and promotional campaigns while preserving one professional visual identity.

Course Completion Outcome

After successful completion, the learner should be able to plan and create professional flyers and posters, structure information clearly, apply grids, typography, colour and imagery, prepare event/promotional/educational materials, adapt designs for digital channels, complete basic print preflight, export suitable files and organise a coordinated campaign handoff.

Professional Practice Checklist

Start with a written brief.

Use one clear primary message.

Keep typography readable at intended size.

Use authorised imagery and licensed assets.

Verify names, dates, prices and contact details.

Confirm printer specifications before commercial printing.

Inspect exported files before publication.

Keep editable masters and approved outputs organised.
