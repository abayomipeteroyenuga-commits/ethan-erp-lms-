SOCIAL MEDIA GRAPHICS

FULL VISUAL STUDY MANUAL

Responsible design standard: use original, authorised or properly licensed assets; keep testimonials, partnerships, prices, dates and promotional claims accurate; protect private information; do not fabricate endorsements or misleading results.

Course Introduction

Social Media Graphics teaches learners to create clear, branded, mobile-first visual content for feed posts, stories, carousels, announcements and promotional campaigns. The course focuses on communication strategy, hierarchy, typography, colour, imagery, accessibility, reusable templates and professional export.

Learning Objectives

Write a useful social graphic brief.

Design for mobile-first platform formats.

Apply hierarchy, grids, typography and colour.

Use images and brand elements responsibly.

Create feed, story and carousel graphics.

Build reusable campaign templates.

Apply accessibility and quality-control checks.

Organise a content-production workflow.

Complete a coordinated multi-format social campaign.
