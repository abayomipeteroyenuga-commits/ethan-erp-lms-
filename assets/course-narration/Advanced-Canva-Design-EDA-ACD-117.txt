ETHAN DIGITAL ACADEMY

ADVANCED CANVA DESIGN

FULL VISUAL STUDY MANUAL

Professional-use standard: create original work or use properly licensed assets; respect privacy, accessibility and brand permissions; do not create misleading endorsements or claims.

Course Introduction

Advanced Canva Design develops the ability to build repeatable visual systems, sophisticated multi-format campaigns and organised professional design workflows. The focus is not on adding more effects; it is on stronger composition, consistent brand rules, reusable templates, information design, motion planning, quality assurance and professional handoff.

Learning Objectives

Build advanced grid-based compositions.

Create reusable typography and colour systems.

Develop brand boards and template families.

Adapt one campaign concept across multiple formats.

Create infographics, carousels, presentations and motion assets.

Apply consistent image art direction.

Use organised review, versioning and handoff workflows.

Design with accessibility and responsible asset use.

Complete a professional multi-format Canva campaign.

After successful completion, the learner should be able to create sophisticated Canva-based visual systems and campaigns, maintain brand consistency across formats, design reusable templates and components, communicate information visually, plan restrained motion, manage feedback and versions, review accessibility and asset rights, and deliver organised professional design packages.

Professional Practice Checklist

Design from a brief, not from decoration.

Use grids and spacing systems consistently.

Define typography and colour roles.

Recompose campaigns for each format.

Use accurate data and authorised assets.

Label conceptual mockups appropriately.

Keep drafts separate from approved exports.

Run accessibility and content checks before publication.

Deliver organised source files, exports and rationale.
