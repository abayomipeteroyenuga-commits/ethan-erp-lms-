LOGO DESIGN FUNDAMENTALS

FULL VISUAL STUDY MANUAL

Originality standard: create original concepts. Do not trace, copy or closely imitate protected logos, trademarks or commercial artwork. For real commercial adoption, appropriate trademark, name and domain checks remain necessary.

Course Introduction

Logo Design Fundamentals teaches the complete beginner-to-intermediate process of developing an original logo system: discovery, research, concept generation, shape and typography decisions, vector construction, colour, variants, testing, usage rules and professional handoff.

Learning Objectives

Interpret a logo design brief.

Distinguish major logo types.

Generate original concepts through structured sketching.

Construct simple vector marks.

Select appropriate typography and colour.

Build responsive logo variants.

Define clear-space and minimum-size rules.

Test logos in realistic contexts.

Prepare an organised professional logo handoff.

Lesson 1: Introduction to Logo Design

Introduction

Introduction to Logo Design develops a specific part of the professional logo-design process. The focus is strategic originality, simplicity, scalability and reliable application rather than decorative effects.

Learning Objectives

Explain the core principles of introduction to logo design.

Complete a guided practical for introduction to logo design.

Apply the lesson to an original education or business identity concept.

Teaching Content

Logo principle: Start with the brand purpose, audience and intended use.

Logo principle: Prefer a clear concept over unnecessary visual complexity.

Logo principle: Keep construction editable and scalable.

Logo principle: Test decisions at small size and in simple one-colour form.

Logo principle: Document approved choices so the logo can be used consistently.

Figure 1: Original instructional visual for Introduction to Logo Design.

Step-by-Step Guided Practical

1. Write the communication objective and three brand keywords.

2. Create at least four small original concept options.

3. Choose one option using the brief rather than decoration preference.

4. Build or refine it using simple editable vector forms and/or authorised typography.

5. Test it at small size, in one colour and on light/dark backgrounds.

Professional Practice Checklist

Research before sketching.

Create original concepts; never trace protected logos.

Prefer clarity and scalability over effects.

Test in one colour and at small size.

Use properly licensed typography and assets.

Create approved logo variants for different spaces.

Document clear space and minimum size.

Keep editable vector masters and organised exports.

Perform appropriate trademark/name checks before real commercial adoption.
