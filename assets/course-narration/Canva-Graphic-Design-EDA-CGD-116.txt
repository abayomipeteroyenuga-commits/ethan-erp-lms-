Course Introduction
Canva Graphic Design teaches visual communication through practical design work. Learners study briefs,
hierarchy, layout, typography, colour, imagery, elements, templates, social graphics, flyers, brand
consistency, presentations, motion, export formats, accessibility and quality control.


Learning Objectives
   Translate a communication brief into a visual design.
   Navigate the Canva workspace confidently.
   Apply hierarchy, alignment, proximity, balance and whitespace.
   Use typography and colour consistently.
   Work responsibly with images, elements and templates.
   Create social media graphics, flyers and multi-page designs.
   Develop a simple brand system.
   Export suitable files for digital or print-oriented use.
   Review designs for accuracy, readability and accessibility.

Lesson 1: Introduction to Graphic Design and
Canva
Introduction
Graphic design is visual communication: arranging text, colour, images, shapes and space so an audience
can understand a message. Canva is a browser- and app-based design platform that helps users create
many common visual materials.


Learning Objectives
   Explain the key principles of introduction to graphic design and canva.
   Complete a guided Canva practical for introduction to graphic design and canva.
   Apply the lesson to an Ethan Digital Academy design scenario.


Teaching Content
Key point: A design should solve a communication problem, not merely look attractive.

Key point: Every design needs a purpose, audience and main message.

Key point: Canva provides templates, text tools, elements, uploads, pages and export options.

Key point: Templates are starting points; professional work still requires judgement, editing and
consistency.

Key point: Originality, accessibility and responsible use of licensed content matter.

Introduction

response.


Learning Objectives
   Explain the key principles of design briefs, audience and purpose.
   Complete a guided Canva practical for design briefs, audience and purpose.

Lesson 4: Visual Hierarchy
Introduction
Visual hierarchy controls the order in which viewers notice and read information.


Learning Objectives
   Explain the key principles of visual hierarchy.
   Complete a guided Canva practical for visual hierarchy.
   Apply the lesson to an Ethan Digital Academy design scenario.


Teaching Content
Key point: Size, weight, colour, contrast and position can signal importance.

Key point: The headline should usually dominate supporting details.

Key point: Too many equally strong elements create competition.

Key point: A call to action should be easy to find.

Key point: Hierarchy should remain clear on a small phone screen.

Lesson 11: Templates and Responsible
Customisation
Introduction
Templates can speed up design but should be adapted to the actual brief and brand.


Learning Objectives
   Explain the key principles of templates and responsible customisation.
   Complete a guided Canva practical for templates and responsible customisation.
   Apply the lesson to an Ethan Digital Academy design scenario.


Teaching Content
Key point: A template is a structure, not a finished identity.

Key point: Replace irrelevant text, colours, images and decorative elements.

Key point: Do not force content into a template that does not suit the message.

Key point: Check that licensed template elements are permitted for the intended use.

Key point: Avoid producing near-identical copies when originality is expected.


Step-by-Step Guided Practical
1. Choose a suitable Canva template.

2. Replace all placeholder text.

3. Apply your practice palette and fonts.

4. Remove unnecessary elements.

5. Compare the result with the original template and explain your changes.


Ethan Digital Academy Case Study
Academy staff can use templates for efficiency while still producing recognisably Ethan-branded work.
