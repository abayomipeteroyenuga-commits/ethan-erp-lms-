ETHAN DIGITAL ACADEMY

ADOBE PHOTOSHOP BASICS

FULL VISUAL STUDY MANUAL

Responsible editing standard: use original, authorised or properly licensed images. Do not alter images to mislead people, fabricate endorsements or expose private information. Keep an original copy and prefer reversible edits.

Course Introduction

Adobe Photoshop Basics introduces raster-image editing through a structured, practical workflow. Learners study the workspace, pixels, file formats, layers, selections, crop, tonal and colour correction, adjustment layers, masks, responsible retouching, text, shapes, blending, Smart Objects, simple compositing and export.

Learning Objectives

Navigate the Photoshop workspace.

Understand pixels, dimensions and common file formats.

Use layers, selections and masks safely.

Correct brightness, contrast and colour non-destructively.

Perform limited responsible cleanup.

Combine images, text and shapes into clear graphics.

Use basic blending and reusable placed content.

Export appropriate files for web and social use.

Complete an organised layered promotional design project.

Professional Practice Checklist

Use only original, authorised or properly licensed assets.

Keep an untouched original or backup.

Prefer adjustment layers and masks to destructive edits.

Name layers and organise files clearly.

Check images at normal viewing size as well as zoomed in.

Keep retouching truthful and natural.

Verify names, dates, prices and claims in promotional graphics.

Keep editable masters separate from approved exports.
