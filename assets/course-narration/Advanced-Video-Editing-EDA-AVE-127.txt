ETHAN DIGITAL ACADEMY

ADVANCED VIDEO EDITING

FULL VISUAL STUDY MANUAL

Responsible editing standard: use original, authorised or properly licensed media; obtain appropriate consent; protect private information; preserve truthful context; do not fabricate testimonials, endorsements, events or evidence; label reenactments/simulations when context requires it.

Course Introduction

Advanced Video Editing develops stronger editorial judgment and finishing skills. Learners move beyond basic trimming into story construction, precision editing, multi-angle concepts, advanced sound, colour matching and grading, keyframed motion, graphics, compositing, speed design, multi-format delivery and professional quality control.

Learning Objectives

Plan an advanced editing workflow and deliverables.

Make story-focused editorial decisions.

Organise larger media projects.

Perform precision timeline editing.

Understand multi-angle editing.

Mix dialogue, music and effects.

Correct and match colour across shots.

Use controlled motion, graphics and compositing.

Master and deliver multi-format video projects.

Lesson 1: Advanced Editing Workflow and Project Planning

Introduction

Advanced Editing Workflow and Project Planning develops professional editorial control. The goal is not to make an edit look complicated, but to make every cut, sound, graphic and visual treatment serve the story, information and intended audience.

Learning Objectives

Explain advanced principles of advanced editing workflow and project planning.

Complete a guided practical for advanced editing workflow and project planning.

Apply the technique to an Ethan Digital Academy production scenario.

Teaching Content

Advanced editing principle: Define the intended viewer, story purpose and final deliverables before detailed editing.

Advanced editing principle: Keep the project organised so revisions can be made without losing source material.

Advanced editing principle: Use advanced techniques only when they improve clarity, continuity, emotion or comprehension.

Advanced editing principle: Compare the edit against the original communication objective during review.

Advanced editing principle: Maintain truthful context, media rights, accessibility and technical quality throughout finishing.

Learning Objectives

Explain advanced principles of accessibility, ethics and responsible editing.

Complete a guided practical for accessibility, ethics and responsible editing.

Apply the technique to an Ethan Digital Academy production scenario.

Teaching Content

Advanced editing principle: Do not edit a person’s words to create a materially false meaning.

Advanced editing principle: Use captions and graphics accurately.

Advanced editing principle: Obtain appropriate permission for private or identifiable material.

Advanced editing principle: Use licensed music, footage, fonts and graphics.

Advanced editing principle: Consider accessibility through captions, readable text and understandable audio.

Course Completion Outcome

After successful completion, the learner should be able to plan and organise an advanced video project, make stronger story-focused editorial decisions, perform precision editing, manage multi-angle concepts, mix audio, correct and grade colour, use keyframed motion and layered graphics, adapt content across formats, apply responsible editing and accessibility principles, and deliver quality-controlled final masters.

Professional Practice Checklist

Plan story and deliverables before detailed editing.

Keep source media and versions organised.

Use advanced techniques only when they improve communication.

Preserve truthful context when editing people and events.

Use authorised/licensed media and obtain appropriate consent.

Keep dialogue, captions and graphics understandable.

Correct before creative colour grading.

Review every final export from beginning to end.

Archive approved masters and project evidence.
